# Raw source data

| Folder | Contents | Provenance | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `leaflets/` | 22 Markdown service leaflets for Firth Valley Health Board, with YAML front matter | Synthetic, written for this project | CC0-1.0 |
| `reference/clinics.csv` | 10 sites and clinics used by the lookup tools | Synthetic, consistent with the leaflets | CC0-1.0 |
| `policies/` | Scottish Government and NHS Scotland policy PDFs | Downloaded from gov.scot and spso.org.uk | Open Government Licence v3.0 (check each document) |

Firth Valley Health Board is fictional. Phone numbers use the Ofcom range reserved for drama (01632 960xxx) and email addresses use the reserved `.example` domain. Real national services (NHS 24, 999, NHS inform, PASS, SPSO, Care Opinion, Breathing Space, Samaritans) are named with their real public contact details.

One leaflet has a superseded 2024 edition (`status: superseded`) on purpose, to test effective-date filtering in retrieval.

No real patient data is stored in this repository.
