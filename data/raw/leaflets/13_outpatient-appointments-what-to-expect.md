# Your outpatient appointment: what to expect

## Before your appointment

- Check your letter or text for the date, time, clinic and site.
- Some appointments are by phone or video. Your letter will say if this is the case.
- Make a list of questions and any medicines you take.

## On the day

- Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time.
- Check in at the clinic reception or the self check-in screen.
- Bring your appointment letter, glasses or hearing aids if you use them, and a list of your medicines.

## Video appointments

Video appointments use NHS Near Me. You need a smartphone, tablet or computer with a camera and an internet connection. You do not need to download an app. Join using the link in your letter about 5 minutes before your appointment.

## Running late

Clinics can run behind schedule. Staff will tell you about any delay. If you are running late, call the clinic on the number on your letter; we may still be able to see you.

## After your appointment

You may be given a follow-up appointment, sent for tests, or discharged back to your GP. A letter is sent to your GP after the appointment and you can ask for a copy.
