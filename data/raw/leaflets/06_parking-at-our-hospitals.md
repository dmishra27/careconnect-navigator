# Parking at our hospitals

Parking is **free** at all Firth Valley Health Board hospital sites.

## Firth Valley Royal Infirmary

- Main visitor car park (Car Park A) next to the main entrance, with 40 accessible spaces
- Car Park C for outpatients, a 5-minute walk from the Outpatients Department
- Drop-off and pick-up zone at the main entrance, maximum 20 minutes
- The car parks are busiest between 10am and 2pm. Please allow extra time.

## Carronside General Hospital

- Visitor car park in front of the main building, with 15 accessible spaces
- Dedicated spaces for Day Surgery patients being collected after treatment

## Inverbrae Community Hospital

- Small car park with 8 accessible spaces. Additional on-street parking is available on Brae Road.

## Blue Badge holders

Accessible spaces are reserved for Blue Badge holders. Please display your badge.

## Electric vehicle charging

Charging points are available in Car Park A at FVRI and at Carronside General Hospital. Charges apply for electricity used.
