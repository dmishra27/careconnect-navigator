# Public transport and patient transport

## By bus

- Routes 8 and 8A run every 15 minutes between Larbank town centre and Firth Valley Royal Infirmary.
- Route 21 links Grangeford, Carronside General Hospital and FVRI every 30 minutes.
- Route 44 runs hourly between Inverbrae and Carronside General Hospital.

## By train

Larbank station is a 20-minute walk or a short bus ride from FVRI.

## Patient Transport Service

The Scottish Ambulance Service provides patient transport for people who cannot travel to hospital any other way **because of their medical condition or mobility needs**. It is not available because you do not have a car or because of the cost of travel.

To check whether you are eligible and to book, use the patient transport booking number printed on your appointment letter. Please book as early as possible and at least 3 working days before your appointment.

## Community transport

Local volunteer car schemes can help people who live in rural areas or find public transport difficult. A small charge may apply. The Patient Services Contact Centre (01632 960 100) can give you details of schemes in your area.

## Help with travel costs

You may be able to claim back the cost of travelling to hospital. See *Help with travel costs*.
