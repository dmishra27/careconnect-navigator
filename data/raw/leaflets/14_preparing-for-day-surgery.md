# Preparing for day surgery

Day surgery means you come into hospital, have your procedure and go home on the same day. Most day surgery takes place at Carronside General Hospital.

## Pre-operative assessment

Before your operation, a nurse will assess your health, usually by phone or at a clinic. Follow the instructions you are given about eating, drinking and your usual medicines.

## Eating and drinking

You will be told when to stop eating and drinking before your operation. Please follow these instructions exactly. If you do not, your operation may need to be cancelled for your safety.

## On the day

- Bring your medicines in their original boxes.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
- Do not bring valuables.
- Remove jewellery and nail varnish.

## Going home

You must have a responsible adult to take you home by car or taxi, not by public transport, and to stay with you for 24 hours after a general anaesthetic. If you do not have anyone, tell the pre-operative assessment team as soon as possible.

## If you become unwell before your operation

If you develop a cough, cold, fever or any new illness in the days before your operation, call the Day Surgery Unit on 01632 960 160 for advice before you travel.
