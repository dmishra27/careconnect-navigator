# Additional support and accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our services. Please tell us if you need support to attend an appointment or a hospital stay.

## Examples of support we can offer

- A longer appointment or the first or last appointment of the day
- A quiet waiting area for people who find busy spaces difficult
- Communication support, including interpreters, Easy Read and hearing loops
- Help for people with a learning disability, dementia or autism, including support from our Learning Disability Liaison Nurses
- Wheelchairs at the main entrance of every hospital
- Space for a carer or support worker to come with you

## Assistance dogs

Assistance dogs are welcome in all our buildings.

## How to ask for support

Call the Patient Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 100, or tell the person who contacts you to book your appointment. We will add a note to your record so the clinic can prepare.

## Carers

If you care for someone, tell staff. You can be involved in conversations about the care of the person you look after if they agree.
