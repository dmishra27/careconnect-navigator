# Giving feedback

We welcome feedback, good or bad. It helps us understand what we do well and what we need to improve.

## Ways to give feedback

- **Care Opinion:** share your story online at Care Opinion, an independent website. Staff read every story and many services respond publicly.
- **Feedback cards:** available on every ward and in every clinic waiting area.
- **Talk to us:** speak to the nurse in charge or the clinic manager.
- **Patient Services Contact Centre:** call 01632 960 100.

## What happens to your feedback

Feedback is shared with the teams involved. We publish a summary each quarter showing what people told us and what we changed as a result.

## Feedback or complaint?

If you would like us to investigate a problem and reply to you, please make a complaint instead. See *Making a complaint*.
