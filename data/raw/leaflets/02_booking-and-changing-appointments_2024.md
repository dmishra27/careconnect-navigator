# Booking and changing your hospital appointment (2024 edition)

This edition has been replaced. The current leaflet should be used.

## Changing your appointment

If you cannot attend, phone the appointment booking line on 01632 960 150 at least 48 hours before your appointment. The booking line is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Please reply to appointment offers within 14 days.

You can change your appointment twice without affecting your place on the waiting list.
