# Booking and changing your hospital appointment

## How you will be offered an appointment

After your GP or another clinician refers you, the hospital team reviews the referral. For most clinics we then contact you to agree a date. You may receive a letter, a text message or a phone call asking you to choose a date and time.

Please reply within **7 days** of being contacted. If we do not hear from you, we will try again. If we still cannot reach you, we may return your referral to the person who referred you.

## Changing or cancelling your appointment

If you cannot attend, tell us as soon as possible so the slot can be offered to someone else.

- Call the Patient Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 100
- Use the link in your appointment text message
- Email appointments@firthvalley.example, quoting the reference number on your letter

You can usually change your appointment **once** without it affecting your place on the waiting list. If you need to change it again, the clinical team will review your referral.

## Reasonable offers

We will offer you a date with at least **7 days' notice**. If you agree to a date at shorter notice, that is fine, but you can turn down a short-notice offer without it affecting your waiting time.

## Telling us about times you are unavailable

If you know you will be unavailable, for example on holiday or recovering from another treatment, tell us when we contact you. Periods when you are unavailable may affect how your waiting time is calculated. See *Waiting times explained*.

## What to have ready when you call

- Your full name and date of birth
- The appointment reference number from your letter or text
- Your CHI number if you know it (this is printed on most NHS letters)
