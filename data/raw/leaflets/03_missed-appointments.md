# What happens if you miss an appointment

Every year thousands of hospital appointments in Firth Valley are missed without notice. Each missed appointment is time that could have been used for another patient.

## If you miss an appointment without telling us

The clinical team will review your referral. Depending on your condition, they may:

- offer you one further appointment, or
- write to you and your GP and return you to your GP's care.

If you are returned to your GP and still need to be seen, your GP can refer you again. A new referral starts a new waiting time.

## When we will always offer another appointment

We will always offer another appointment, rather than returning you to your GP, if:

- you are a child or young person under 16
- you have been referred with a suspected cancer
- the clinician decides it is not safe to discharge you

## If something prevents you from attending

Things happen. If you missed an appointment because of an emergency, illness or a problem with transport, call us on 01632 960 100 as soon as you can. Tell us if you need support to attend, for example an interpreter, transport or a carer to come with you.
