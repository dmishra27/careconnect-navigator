# When to call NHS 24 or 999

The Patient Services Contact Centre cannot give medical advice and is not an emergency service.

## Call 999 immediately for a life-threatening emergency

Examples include:

- Chest pain or pressure, especially if it spreads to the arm, neck or jaw
- Difficulty breathing or choking
- Signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, speech problems. Time to call 999.
- Severe bleeding that will not stop
- Loss of consciousness, a seizure that does not stop, or sudden confusion
- A serious head injury
- Someone who is in immediate danger of harming themselves

## Call NHS 24 on 111 when you need urgent care that cannot wait

NHS 24 is free and available 24 hours a day. Call 111 if you are ill and cannot wait for your GP surgery to open. NHS 24 can also connect you to mental health support (choose the mental health option when you call).

## Other places to get help

- **NHS inform** has trusted health information and a symptom checker.
- **Your local pharmacy** can give advice and treatment for many common conditions.
- **Minor Injuries Unit** at Carronside General Hospital for sprains, cuts and minor burns, open 8am to 9pm.
- **Your GP practice** for health problems that are not urgent.

## Emotional support

- Breathing Space: 0800 83 85 87, free and confidential
- Samaritans: 116 123, 24 hours a day
