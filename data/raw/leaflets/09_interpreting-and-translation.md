# Interpreting and translation services

Interpreting and translation are **free** for all patients, carers and parents. You should not have to rely on family or friends to interpret, and children should never be asked to interpret.

## Spoken language interpreters

We can provide an interpreter in person, by phone or by video for appointments, hospital stays and calls with our staff. Tell us which language you need when you are first contacted, or call 01632 960 100 and ask for an interpreter to be booked. Please give at least 3 working days' notice for face-to-face interpreters.

## British Sign Language (BSL)

We can book a BSL/English interpreter for your appointment. BSL users can also contact us through contactSCOTLAND-BSL, the free national video relay service.

## Translated information

We can provide letters and leaflets in other languages, large print, Braille and Easy Read on request.

## Emergency situations

In the Emergency Department, staff can connect to a telephone interpreter within minutes, 24 hours a day.

## Let us know your needs

We record your communication needs so that you do not have to repeat them each time. Please tell us if they change.
