# Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with any of our services, you can make a complaint. Making a complaint will not affect your care.

## Who can complain

Patients, carers, relatives and anyone affected by our services can complain. Someone can complain on your behalf with your permission.

## Time limits

Please complain as soon as possible, normally within **6 months** of the event or of finding out you have a reason to complain.

## How we handle complaints

We follow the NHS Scotland Model Complaints Handling Procedure, which has two stages.

- **Stage 1, early resolution:** we aim to resolve simple complaints quickly, usually within **5 working days**, often by talking with you.
- **Stage 2, investigation:** for complex or serious complaints, or if you are unhappy with the stage 1 response, we investigate and aim to send you a full written response within **20 working days**. If we need longer, we will tell you why and agree a new timescale.

## If you are still unhappy

After stage 2, you can ask the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman (SPSO) to look at your complaint. The SPSO is independent and free.

## How to complain

- Speak to a member of staff
- Phone the Complaints Team on 01632 960 180
- Email complaints@firthvalley.example
- Write to the Complaints Team, Firth Valley Royal Infirmary, Royal Infirmary Road, Larbank FV1 4QX

The Patient Advice and Support Service can help you make a complaint.
