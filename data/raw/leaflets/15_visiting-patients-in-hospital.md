# Visiting patients in hospital

We support person-centred visiting. There are no fixed visiting hours on most wards. Family and friends can visit at times that suit the patient.

## Things to know

- Ward staff may ask visitors to leave for a short time during care, ward rounds or protected mealtimes.
- Please keep to two visitors at a bedside at any time, unless ward staff agree otherwise.
- Children are welcome but must be supervised by an adult.
- Intensive care and some specialist wards have their own arrangements. Please call the ward before visiting.

## Infection control

Please do not visit if you have had diarrhoea, vomiting, a fever or flu-like symptoms in the last 48 hours. Clean your hands when you enter and leave the ward.

## Bringing items for patients

Pyjamas, toiletries, phone chargers and books are welcome. Please check with ward staff before bringing food. Flowers are not allowed on some wards.

## Contacting a ward

Use the switchboard on 01632 960 000 and ask for the ward by name. For privacy, staff may give detailed information only to the patient's named contact.
