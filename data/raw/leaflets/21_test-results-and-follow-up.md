# Test results and follow-up

## How you will get your results

The clinician who asked for the test will tell you how you will receive your results. This may be at a follow-up appointment, by letter, by phone, or through your GP.

## How long results take

- Most blood test results are available to the requesting clinician within 2 to 3 working days.
- X-ray and scan reports usually take 1 to 3 weeks.
- Some specialised tests take longer.

## If you have not heard

If you were told to expect results and have not heard within the expected time, contact the clinic or the person who asked for the test. The Patient Services Contact Centre (01632 960 100) can put you in touch with the right team but cannot give you results over the phone.

## Urgent results

If a result needs urgent action, a clinician will contact you directly. Please make sure we have your current phone number and address.
