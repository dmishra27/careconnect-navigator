# Help with travel costs

You may be able to claim back the cost of travelling to an NHS hospital appointment.

## Who can claim

You can usually claim if you or your partner receive certain benefits, for example Income Support, income-based Jobseeker's Allowance, income-related Employment and Support Allowance, Pension Credit Guarantee Credit, or Universal Credit within the income limits. You may also qualify through the NHS Low Income Scheme, which uses form HC1.

## What you can claim for

- The cheapest public transport fare
- Mileage for car journeys, at the standard NHS rate, plus parking where it applies
- Travel for an escort if your clinician says you need someone with you

## How to claim

1. Keep your tickets and receipts.
2. Bring proof of your benefit or your HC2 certificate to your appointment.
3. Ask at the Outpatients reception desk for a travel claim form, or claim within 3 months by sending the form and receipts to the Patient Travel Team.

Patient Travel Team, Firth Valley Royal Infirmary, Royal Infirmary Road, Larbank FV1 4QX. Phone 01632 960 140.

## If you are not sure

Call the Patient Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 100. Staff can explain the rules and send you the forms.
