# How referrals from your GP work

## What happens after your GP refers you

1. Your GP sends an electronic referral to the hospital.
2. A specialist reviews the referral, usually within 5 working days. This is called triage.
3. The specialist decides the most suitable next step. This may be an appointment at a clinic, a test first, advice sent back to your GP, or a referral to another service.
4. You will be contacted with the outcome.

## Urgent referrals

If your GP refers you with a suspected cancer, the referral is marked urgent and is reviewed as a priority. You should be contacted quickly with an appointment. Please make sure your GP has your current phone number.

## Active Clinical Referral Triage

Sometimes the specialist decides you do not need a hospital appointment and sends advice to your GP instead, or arranges a test. This can mean you get the right care sooner.

## Checking on your referral

If you have not heard anything within 4 weeks of your GP referral, call the Patient Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 100. We can confirm that the referral has been received and tell you its status. We cannot tell you the clinical reason for a triage decision; your GP or the clinical team can discuss that with you.
