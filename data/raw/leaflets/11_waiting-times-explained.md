# Waiting times explained

## National standards in Scotland

- **Treatment Time Guarantee:** if you are eligible, you should start inpatient or day case treatment within **12 weeks** of agreeing to the treatment with your clinician. This is set out in the Patient Rights (Scotland) Act 2011.
- **New outpatient appointments:** the national standard is that patients wait no longer than **12 weeks** for a first outpatient appointment.
- **18 weeks referral to treatment:** the national standard is that patients start treatment within **18 weeks** of referral.

## How your waiting time is counted

Your waiting time starts when the hospital receives your referral, or for the Treatment Time Guarantee, when you and your clinician agree to the treatment. If you turn down a reasonable offer, or tell us you are unavailable for a period, your waiting time may be adjusted. We will always tell you if this happens.

## Current waits

Waiting times vary by specialty. The Patient Services Contact Centre can tell you the current typical wait for a specialty, but cannot guarantee a date. Public Health Scotland publishes official waiting times statistics every month.

## If you are worried about your wait

- If your symptoms get worse, contact your GP, who can ask the hospital to review your referral.
- If you think your wait is longer than it should be, call 01632 960 100. You can also contact the Patient Advice and Support Service for independent help.
