# Accessing your health records

You have the right to see and get a copy of the information we hold about you. This is called a subject access request.

## How to ask

- Complete the access request form, available from the Patient Services Contact Centre (01632 960 100), or
- Write to the Health Records Access Team, Firth Valley Royal Infirmary, Royal Infirmary Road, Larbank FV1 4QX, or
- Email healthrecords@firthvalley.example

Please tell us which records you want, for example records from a particular hospital, specialty or time period.

## Proof of identity

We will ask for proof of identity before releasing records, to protect your privacy.

## How long it takes

We aim to respond within **one month**. In some complex cases this may be extended, and we will tell you if so.

## Cost

There is normally no charge.

## Asking for records about someone else

You can ask for records about someone else only if you have their written permission or legal authority, such as Power of Attorney. Parents can usually ask for records about young children.
