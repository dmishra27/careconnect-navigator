# Our hospital sites and opening hours

## Firth Valley Royal Infirmary (FVRI)

- Address: Royal Infirmary Road, Larbank FV1 4QX
- Emergency Department: open 24 hours, every day
- Outpatient clinics: Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, some evening clinics until 8pm
- Main reception and switchboard: 01632 960 000

## Carronside General Hospital

- Address: Carron Way, Grangeford FV3 2LP
- Minor Injuries Unit: open every day 8am to 9pm for injuries such as sprains, minor burns, cuts and suspected simple fractures. It does not treat chest pain, breathing problems or serious illness.
- Day Surgery Unit: Monday to Friday 7am to 8pm
- Outpatient clinics: Monday to Friday 8.30am to 5.30pm

## Inverbrae Community Hospital

- Address: Brae Road, Inverbrae FV7 9DE
- Outpatient clinics, X-ray and physiotherapy: Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm
- X-ray walk-in (with a GP request form): Monday to Friday 9am to 12 noon
- There is no Emergency Department or Minor Injuries Unit at Inverbrae.

## Public holidays

Outpatient clinics are closed on public holidays. The Emergency Department at FVRI stays open. If your appointment falls on a public holiday, we will contact you to rearrange it.
