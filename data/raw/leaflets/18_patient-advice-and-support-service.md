# Patient Advice and Support Service (PASS)

The Patient Advice and Support Service is an independent, free and confidential service for people who use NHS services in Scotland. It is delivered by Citizens Advice.

## How PASS can help

- Explain your rights and responsibilities as a patient
- Help you give feedback, make a comment, raise a concern or make a complaint
- Support you to prepare for meetings about your care
- Direct you to other services, such as advocacy or money advice

## What PASS does not do

PASS does not give medical or legal advice, and it is not part of Firth Valley Health Board.

## Contacting PASS

You can contact PASS through your local Citizens Advice Bureau. The Patient Services Contact Centre (01632 960 100) can give you local contact details.
