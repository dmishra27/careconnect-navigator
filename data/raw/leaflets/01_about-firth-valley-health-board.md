# About Firth Valley Health Board

Firth Valley Health Board provides hospital and community health services for around 400,000 people across Larbank, Grangeford, Inverbrae and the surrounding towns and villages.

## Our hospitals

- **Firth Valley Royal Infirmary (FVRI), Larbank** is our main acute hospital. It has the Emergency Department, maternity unit, intensive care and most specialist outpatient clinics.
- **Carronside General Hospital, Grangeford** provides planned surgery, day surgery, outpatient clinics, a Minor Injuries Unit and rehabilitation wards.
- **Inverbrae Community Hospital, Inverbrae** provides outpatient clinics, X-ray, physiotherapy and inpatient care for people recovering closer to home.

## Community services

We run 22 community clinics offering services such as podiatry, physiotherapy, sexual health, diabetes education, blood tests and child health. See the leaflet *Community clinics directory* for locations and opening hours.
