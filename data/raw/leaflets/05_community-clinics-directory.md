# Community clinics directory

This leaflet lists our most used community clinics. A full list of all 22 clinics is available from the Patient Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 100.

## Larbank area

- **Larbank Health Centre**, Market Street, Larbank. Blood tests, podiatry, diabetes education. Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm.
- **Westfield Clinic**, Westfield Avenue, Larbank. Child health, health visiting, baby clinics. Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm.

## Grangeford area

- **Grangeford Community Clinic**, Dock Road, Grangeford. Physiotherapy, podiatry, blood tests. Monday to Friday 8.30am to 5pm, Tuesday late clinic until 7pm.
- **Harbourside Sexual Health Clinic**, Quay Street, Grangeford. Sexual health and contraception. Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 10am to 7pm. Walk-in sessions on Wednesday afternoons.

## Inverbrae area

- **Inverbrae Health Centre**, Station Road, Inverbrae. Blood tests, podiatry, speech and language therapy. Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm.

## Rural clinics

- **Kilbrannan Village Clinic**, Kilbrannan. Nurse-led clinic for dressings, blood tests and injections. Tuesday and Thursday 9am to 1pm.
- **Muirhead Clinic**, Muirhead. Podiatry and physiotherapy. Monday and Friday 9am to 4pm.

## Booking

Most community clinics need an appointment. Blood test appointments can be booked online through the link on your request form or by calling 01632 960 120.
